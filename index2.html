What is 30 Minute Singer?

30 Minute Singer is a singing course for busy people that grows your range, tone, pitch, control, and stamina… by respecting your time and telling you exactly what to do each day to achieve impressive results.

It works even if you have no natural singing talent, because the exercises inside target the 3 muscle groups responsible for making the voice sound good or bad (larynx, diaphragm, intercostals)… “reprogramming” them to sound good.

It works even if you are 60+ (we have students in their 70s killing it), for any genre including pop, R&B, hip-hop, theater, rock, metal, jazz, funk, and everything in-between.
